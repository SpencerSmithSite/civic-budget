CivicBudget mockups

Eight screens for the Phase 4.5 admin design pass and the Phase 5 portal, drawn from the approved design brief: a modern civic ERP that fits in next to VIP. Every number is Village of Maple Ridge seed data. Scroll each frame sideways if your window is narrower than 1200px.

The shell: dark sidebar with module groups (the VIP mental model, one generation newer), a white top bar with breadcrumb and user, KPI cards that lead with the budget in progress, then the versions table and an audit timeline. The over-limit fund is the one thing colored on the page.

6. Public portal, overview

Question-led sections, three KPI cards, sorted horizontal bars with direct labels and a $ | % toggle, a table twin and download on every chart, and Ohio Checkbook-style trust chrome (seal, resolution, published date, data source, glossary, accessibility statement). Larger type than the admin app: citizens read, staff scan.
